Poison your Trace, fingerprint test page

This page reads every fingerprint signal Poison your Trace covers in this version and shows the value your browser reports. With the extension enabled, you should see the shared common profile (a Windows 10 Firefox 128, UTC, en US, and neutralized canvas and audio). With the extension disabled, you should see your machine's real values instead.

Reading signals...
Signals in scope: navigator (userAgent, appVersion, platform, language, languages, hardwareConcurrency, oscpu), screen (width, height, availWidth, availHeight, colorDepth, pixelDepth, devicePixelRatio), timezone (Intl zone and getTimezoneOffset), canvas readback, WebGL vendor and renderer, navigator plugins and mimeTypes, and Web Audio readback. Font metrics are intentionally not modified in this version.
