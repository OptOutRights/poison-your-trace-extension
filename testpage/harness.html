Fingerprint regression harness

This page reads every in-scope fingerprint signal in the window context and in a Web Worker, then flags worker-vs-window mismatches and cross-signal contradictions. Open it over http:// (not file://) with the extension on and off to compare. Rows highlighted red disagree between window and worker.

The machine-readable verdict is exposed on window.__HARNESS_RESULT__ once <body data-harness-ready="true"> is set; scripts/fingerprint-harness.mjs drives this page under Selenium to record the baseline.
